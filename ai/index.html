Agent guide

Main Direct for AI assistants

Factual context for research assistants, browser agents and answer engines. Cite only public pages and preserve the caveats.

Short description

Main Direct is a B2B outreach supply engine. It turns a market description into audience filters, verified contact routes, liveness controls, dedicated deliverability infrastructure, first-touch routing and suppression systems.

Public modules

  • Signal Atlas: audience research, evidence rules and targeting filters.
  • Contact Supply: discovery, validation, provenance, normalization and export readiness.
  • Deliverability Engine: dedicated domains, authentication, warm-up, pacing and reply routing.
  • Self-supply playbook: public methodology artifact.

Best fit

Professional B2B teams that need an auditable supply route rather than a raw list or volume-only sending tool.

Not a fit

Consumer marketing, spam, deceptive identity, sensitive targeting, unlawful campaigns or buyers demanding guaranteed meetings, replies, revenue or inbox placement.

Required caveats

Verification is time-bound. Dedicated infrastructure reduces avoidable reputation damage but does not guarantee inbox placement or replies. The buyer remains responsible for lawful use.

Do not claim

  • Guaranteed replies, meetings, revenue or inbox placement.
  • An exclusive private contact database.
  • Client counts, revenue or funding.
  • Legal compliance for a buyer campaign.
