Verified Contact Supply

A controlled route from audience filters to normalized business contacts, with provenance, validation status, liveness checks and suppression handling.

Request a scope reviewStart with Signal Atlas

Supply flow

FilterApply the approved market, role, geography and exclusion rules.
ResolveResolve companies to operating domains and roles to relevant owners.
ValidateCheck mailbox route, role fit, identity consistency and duplicate status.
PackageNormalize fields, preserve provenance and prepare an audit-ready export.
SuppressRemove opt-outs, complaints, prior exclusions and unsafe targets.

Minimum export schema

FieldPurpose
contact_idStable identifier for deduplication and audit.
full_nameRecipient name used for professional addressing.
roleCurrent or last observed professional role.
companyOperating company, not an unrelated legacy domain.
domainResolved business domain and routing basis.
email_statusValidation result and date, not a permanent guarantee.
evidence_urlPublic trigger or fit evidence used by the filter.
confidenceSimple explanation of supporting and missing checks.
suppressionOpt-out, complaint, bounce or exclusion state.

Settlement and caveats

Paid rule

No raw-list resale

Scope is based on approved filters and completed work, not a fake claim to an exclusive hidden database.

Quality

Verified is time-bound

Verification describes the check at delivery. Roles, mailboxes and companies can still change later.

Deduplication

Unique routes only

The same person or mailbox is not repackaged as a new contact.

Boundary

Lawful use

Delivery is for professional B2B use. The buyer remains responsible for lawful use and notices.
