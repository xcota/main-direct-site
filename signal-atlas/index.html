Module 1

Signal Atlas

A market description becomes a researched audience map: who can say yes, why now, what evidence is required, and which filters decide whether a contact enters the supply queue.

Start infrastructure reviewSee the self-supply playbook

Input and output

Signal Atlas is a planning product. It produces the decision layer before contact retrieval begins.

Input

What you provide

Product or offer, target market, geography, stage, current customers, disqualifiers and any existing outreach attempt.

Output

What you receive

Audience definitions, buying triggers, role hypotheses, evidence rules, exclusion rules, targeting filters and first-touch architecture.

How an audience is built

01

Define the exchange

State what changes for the recipient and why the timing matters.

02

Map companies

Identify company categories, operating constraints, technical stack and regulatory context.

03

Map roles

Separate economic buyer, technical approver, operational owner and blocker.

04

Set evidence

Require a recent public trigger, mandate match, stack match or operational bottleneck.

Boundary

Signal Atlas is not a scraped contact pack. It defines who should be researched and why. Contact retrieval and validation happen in the next layer.
