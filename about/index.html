The supply side of B2B outreach

Main Direct is an independent B2B outreach supply engine for audience filters, contact validation, liveness and deliverability infrastructure. It is not a list broker or volume-only agency.

About

Built around one constraint

Most outreach systems do not fail because someone wrote a bad sentence. They fail because the contact was irrelevant, stale, unreachable or sent through infrastructure that could not carry it.
