Privacy

This site has no analytics, advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. The contact form uses a delivery processor to route submissions.

What we collect

The contact form collects only the details you submit: name, work email, company or project, website, target segment, geography, current data, sending setup and your stated constraint. We use them to assess the request and reply.

Delivery processing

Static hosting cannot receive form posts directly. Submissions are routed through a form-delivery processor to Main Direct’s private mailbox. The processor acts as a delivery intermediary for this request and is not used to profile you or add you to a mailing list.

Site hosting

Static files are served by GitHub Pages. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request logs for availability, security and abuse prevention.

No tracking

The site has no analytics cookies, advertising pixels, session replay or cross-site tracking. Your browser may store standard form-input data locally until you close or clear it.

Data sharing

Request contents are not sold or shared for advertising. Operational data stays within the systems needed to deliver the work.

Retention

Correspondence is retained only while useful for the request, project or legal record. Reply through the original thread if you need deletion of non-essential correspondence.
