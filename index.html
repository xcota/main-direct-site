Product modules

Three supply modules, one control layer

Outreach breaks in different places. A weak list wastes research; a bad domain burns messages; an unfocused first touch burns attention. Main Direct builds the system end to end.

Module 1

Signal Atlas

Market description becomes audience filters, evidence requirements, disqualifiers and first-touch angles. Planning first; contacts second.

Module 2

Contact Supply

Company-domain resolution, mailbox-pattern testing, independent identity checks, normalization, provenance and a CRM-ready schema.

Module 3

Deliverability Engine

Dedicated sending domains, SPF/DKIM/DMARC architecture, warm-up gates, pacing, reputation controls, suppression logic and reply routing.

Layer 4

First-touch systems

Signal capture, qualification filters, message architecture and routing so the first touch is relevant, small and testable.

Control

Observability

Per-domain volume, authentication, bounce classes, complaints, seed placement, replies and suppression status in one operational view.

Boundary

Not a list broker

Main Direct does not sell scraped exports or promise inbox placement. It builds systems where the filter, evidence and infrastructure are visible.

Operating model

Signal to inbox, without guesswork

The path is deliberately boring. Each stage has an owner, an exit condition and an audit trail.

DefineMarket, segment, role, geography and disqualification rules.
ResolveName and company become a domain, organization and likely route.
ValidateMailbox, role, identity and liveness are checked independently.
DeliverAuthenticated domain, controlled volume and reputation guardrails.
LearnBounces, placement, replies and signals update the next wave.

Principles

Infrastructure first

The useful asset is not a raw export. It is the system that can tell which contacts are worth attention and which routes can carry them.

Filter before sendThe correct answer is often fewer contacts, not more volume.
Separate identitiesCold infrastructure stays isolated from brand-critical domains.
Provenance mattersEvery contact keeps a source, validation step and freshness signal.
No invented proofNo fabricated case studies, metrics or inbox-placement promises.
