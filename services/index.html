Signal and audience engineering

Build a source-of-truth pipeline instead of forwarding spreadsheets.

Input

Source mapping

Identify public registries, filings, funding records, event data, technical artifacts and owned data that can produce relevant companies and roles.

Structure

Canonical schema

Normalize person, organization, domain, role, geography, source, confidence and freshness fields before enrichment begins.

Output

Routing-ready base

Remove duplicates, dead branches and irrelevant titles; export only records that meet the campaign entry rule.

Contact validation & enrichment

A plausible email is not a contact. Validation happens before campaign access.

Domain

Company resolution

Resolve company names to the correct operating domain and reject lookalikes, subsidiaries and unrelated legacy domains.

Mailbox

Route testing

Derive and test the smallest defensible set of mailbox patterns using provider responses, public evidence and role constraints.

Identity

Person confirmation

Cross-check title, employer, public profile and other independent signals to reduce namesakes and stale titles.

Email deliverability infrastructure

Make sending infrastructure boring, observable and recoverable.

Domains

Isolated sending identities

Separate cold sending from the brand domain, with dedicated subdomains, root domains and rollback boundaries.

Authentication

SPF, DKIM & DMARC

Design DNS authentication for each sending identity, align headers where required and monitor failures without breaking legitimate mail.

Ramp

Reputation control

Start with low volume, stable templates and real engagement; expand only after bounce, complaint and placement signals remain acceptable.

First-touch architecture

The first message is a diagnostic, not a volume test.

Step 1

Qualify

Apply segment, role, geography and timing filters. Disqualification is part of the design.

Step 2

Signal

Require a current, specific reason why the conversation matters now.

Step 3

Compress

Answer why-now, relevance, proof and next step in the first 80 words.

Step 4

Route

Send from the correct identity, log responses and move qualified replies out of automation.
